Research

What we study

  1. 01

    Capability

    How general intelligence scales, and what limits it. We measure what systems can do before we guess.

  2. 02

    Alignment

    How to make a mind more capable than ours act in our interest. This holds as it grows.

  3. 03

    Understanding

    How these systems work on the inside. We cannot trust what we cannot explain.

Principles

How we work

  • Careful

    We move at the speed of evidence.

    Each step is tested before the next one is taken.

  • Open

    We publish what we learn.

    Methods, results, and mistakes, so others can check our work.

  • Long-horizon

    We work on a scale of decades.

    The questions that matter most will not be settled quickly.
